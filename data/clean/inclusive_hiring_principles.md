# Inclusive hiring principles

- Use gender-neutral and respectful language.
- Define observable competencies and outcomes.
- Replace vague culture-fit requirements with role-relevant collaboration criteria.
- Separate must-have requirements from nice-to-have preferences.
- Avoid assumptions about caregivers, remote workers, age, gender, or background.
- Use evidence-based interview rubrics and structured scoring.
- Evaluate leadership through behavior and impact, not style stereotypes.
