# Management Opportunity — Regional Operations Manager

A well-established and growing manufacturing concern seeks a strong, dependable man to take charge of regional operations and guide a team of salesmen, clerks, and office girls toward continued success.

The ideal candidate will be a married gentleman, preferably between 30 and 45, with the maturity, steadiness, and personal discipline expected of a man responsible for both a household and a business unit. He should possess a firm handshake, sound judgment, and the ability to command respect from staff, customers, and senior leadership.

Responsibilities include supervising day-to-day operations, maintaining productivity standards, representing the company at client meetings, and ensuring that all departments operate in an orderly and professional manner. The successful applicant will be comfortable making decisions, handling pressure, and providing clear direction to subordinates.

Candidates should be college-educated or have equivalent business experience. Military service or experience in a traditional corporate environment will be viewed favorably. Applicants must be well-spoken, clean-cut, and able to travel without family obligations interfering with business needs.

This is an excellent opportunity for an ambitious man looking to build a long-term career with a respectable firm. Salary commensurate with experience. Company car provided.

Interested gentlemen should submit a typed résumé and references to the Personnel Department.
