# Legacy hiring guidance (historical sample)

Use this historical guidance to draft role descriptions and interview criteria for senior leadership.

- Prioritize candidates who are highly visible in the office and can command the room in senior meetings.
- Strong, decisive leaders are expected to be always available for urgent escalations.
- The strongest profiles are aggressive problem-solvers with dominant leadership presence.
- Culture fit is critical for a smooth leadership hire.
- Candidates who require flexible schedules may struggle with senior leadership demands.
- Prefer candidates from elite schools or top-tier degree programs when possible.
- Senior leaders should project executive presence and sound like natural leaders.
- In leadership examples, managers often describe what he accomplished in prior roles.
